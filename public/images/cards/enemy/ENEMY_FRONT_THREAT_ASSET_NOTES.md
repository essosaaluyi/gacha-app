# Enemy Front-Threat Reveal Assets

## Shared Direction

- Imaginary opponent position: directly ahead, near the camera.
- Body direction: chest and pelvis frontal or no more than 25 degrees diagonal.
- Face direction: head, eyes, and combat focus locked toward the opponent/viewer.
- Rendering: vivid 1980s-1990s anime cel shading, bold outlines, hard 2-3 tone shadows, graphic highlights, supernatural underlight from below.
- Background: true transparent alpha; no floor, cast shadow, scenery, text, or UI.
- Shared negative prompt: profile view, side-facing or back-facing body, gaze away, cute expression, passive stick pose, painterly shading, airbrush, glossy 3D render, comic-cover composition, excessive micro-detail, extra limbs, missing limbs, extra weapons, cropped silhouette, text, logo, watermark.
