# UR3

Type: Player card
Character name: UR3
Description: A forgotten doll rebooted by dreads and abandoned memories. It wanders the alleys of the night city, half-lost and half-awake, searching for a purpose it no longer understands.
Ability: Each round predetermines a random attack rate between 70% and 100%. After round 2, Abandoned Doll has a 50% chance to self-destruct, and destruction checks happen before other draw effects.
