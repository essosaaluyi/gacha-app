# UR1

Type: Player card
Character name: UR1
Description: A dimension traveler searching across worlds for her missing brother. Her lure is emotional: she feels like a main heroine with a personal mission bigger than the battle.
Ability: If Mami draws Chance, enemy defeat is predetermined. After the bonus round, Mami is guaranteed to die: on the first round-insert draw the next card becomes internally active, while Mami remains visually displayed until her destruction animation on the next visible game. After that, the next draw is guaranteed triple Bar.
