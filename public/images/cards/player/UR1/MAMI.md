# UR1

Type: Player card
Character name: UR1
Description: A dimension traveler searching across worlds for her missing brother. Her lure is emotional: she feels like a main heroine with a personal mission bigger than the battle.
Ability: After Mami completes a full Bonus and Pick a Bonus cycle, the next three main-battle games use boosted BAR flags: triple BAR at 1/11 and BAR fakeout at 1/8, rolled independently with triple BAR taking priority. The boost begins with the first Draw after the next round insert, also applies in fatal mode, and ends after three games or immediately when another bonus opening begins. If a bonus stock is queued, the boost waits until the stock queue is empty.
