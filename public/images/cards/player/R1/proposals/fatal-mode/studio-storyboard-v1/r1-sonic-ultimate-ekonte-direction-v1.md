# R1 Triplets Baby Dragon Sonic Ultimate

## Studio Storyboard Direction v1

Format: 15 seconds, 16:9, 24 fps, 360 frames

Production intent: professional Japanese animation e-konte and layout package. The Dreamina video is motion reference only. R1 reference art controls identity. The speed-line background, nest, characters, contact shadows, and sonic pressure are separate production layers.

## Global Continuity Lock

- Exactly three identical R1 baby dragons whenever the trio is visible.
- Cream-white compact bodies, huge amber eyes, cyan crystal crests and back spikes, orange inner ear fins, cyan markings, four short legs, and one tail each.
- No foreground tail, extra dragon, enemy, egg, textured floor, horizon, rubble, smoke tunnel, beam, ring, bubble, dome, or spherical wave.
- Screen direction remains left to right from the release through the enemy-scene handoff.
- The camera stays outside the trio formation. No lens move through bodies.
- Background is an abstract black anime speed-line field. Floor is seamless matte black with contact shadows only.
- Sonic pressure is a broad, flat, mostly invisible wall shown with broken refraction, white pressure cuts, and restrained cyan accents.

## Sheet A: Nest Emergence And Launch, 0:00-3:14

### A01 | 0:00-0:11 | 11f | FIX | 85mm macro | camera 8 cm above nest rim

Picture: The woven rim cuts across the lower third as a heavy dark silhouette. Three cyan crystal tips rise at three distinct depths. Keep the trio hidden.

Action: Nest twitches once, then stops. Crystal tips move only two or three centimeters.

Layers: BG speed lines at 5 percent density, NEST foreground, CHAR tips, no FX.

Sound: One dry wicker creak. Low sub-bass begins.

### A02 | 0:11-0:24 | 13f | slow T.U. | 65mm | slight low angle from outside nest

Picture: Three pairs of amber eyes appear under the rim. Center pair is higher; flank pairs sit lower and wider. Use negative black around the eyes.

Action: Eyes open left, right, then center, two frames apart.

Layers: Sparse inward speed lines remain behind the nest. No floor.

Sound: Three tiny inhale clicks, then silence.

### A03 | 0:24-1:11 | 11f | FIX | 35mm | low three-quarter

Picture: Reveal the three compact bodies compressed inside the nest. Forelegs brace the rim. The nest is an ellipse in perspective, never a flat circle.

Action: All three squash downward. Wicker sidewalls bow out slightly and recover halfway.

Animation: Strong anticipation key at final frame. Tails stay contained behind each body.

### A04 | 1:11-2:03 | 16f | fast T.B. plus 8-degree tilt recovery | 24mm low angle

Picture: The trio launches above the nest in a triangular arrangement. Center dragon is highest and farther back. Flank dragons arc forward left and right. Camera remains outside their paths.

Action: Six forelegs clear the rim. Nest rebounds once. Three to five straw arcs only.

FX: PREPARE speed lines accelerate outward. No debris, dust, or floor fragments.

### A05 | 2:03-2:20 | 17f | overhead crane down, rotate 8 degrees to level | 28mm

Picture: Top-down view of the trio descending toward three clean landing marks. Their bodies form an arrowhead pointing screen right. Nest falls away into black at upper left.

Action: Limbs extend for landing. Tails trail behind their own bodies without crossing.

Continuity: Establish left-to-right attack direction here.

### A06 | 2:20-3:14 | 18f | floor-level FIX with two-frame impact shake | 32mm

Picture: Triangular landing on matte-black floor. Center dragon lands half a body-length behind the flankers. Horizon is invisible.

Action: Feet contact two frames apart, then synchronize into one low recoil. Contact shadows pulse; no dust.

Sound: Three soft impacts resolving into one bass hit.

## Sheet C: Inhale And Sonic Release, 5:19-9:19

### C01 | 5:19-6:07 | 12f | slow T.U. begins | 85mm telephoto

Picture: Clean three-face composition. Each face occupies a distinct third. No overlap across eyes or crests.

Action: Tiny central intake at each mouth. Cheeks remain anatomically normal.

Speed lines: Thin strokes move inward from all edges toward the space between faces.

### C02 | 6:07-7:00 | 17f | accelerating T.U. | 95mm

Picture: Frame compresses around the trio. Outer heads approach the side edges but defining silhouettes remain intact.

Action: Cheeks expand from 0 to 7 percent. Throat scales stretch minimally. Pupils sharpen.

Animation: No balloon lobes and no glossy inflation.

### C03 | 7:00-7:17 | 17f | T.U. reaches maximum | 110mm

Picture: Intense close-up of all three eyes, noses, and mouth corners. Cyan crests break the top frame only at nonessential tips.

Action: Cheeks reach 12-15 percent maximum. Bodies brace below frame. Inward lines accelerate.

Sound: Rising air suction and tightening sub tone.

### C04 | 7:17-8:05 | 12f | FIX | 110mm

Picture: Dead-still close hold. Near-total black surrounds the faces. Inward lines freeze in place.

Action: No blinking. No cheek growth. Hold the exact drawing for 12 frames except a one-pixel crystal shimmer.

Sound: Complete silence for the final 6 frames.

### C05 | 8:05-8:10 | 5f | impact drawing | virtual 18mm

Picture: One graphic black-white-cyan reversal frame. Three mouth shapes remain readable inside a shared pressure flash.

Action: Left, center, right release at two-frame offsets, then lock into one event.

FX: Direction changes from inward to outward. No beam cores or circular rings.

### C06 | 8:10-9:19 | 33f | explosive T.B. to ultra-wide | 18mm to 28mm feel

Picture: Camera retreats from close-up to floor-level wide. Trio becomes small at left-center while staying readable. The attack leaves as one shared flat pressure onset.

Action: Torsos recoil, feet stay planted, fins and tails lag backward.

FX: One broken vertical refraction edge begins forming. Outward speed-line burst remains behind characters.

### D04 | 12:02-13:02 | 24f | cut ahead, camera faces incoming front | 70mm

### D05 | 13:02-13:16 | 14f | pressure overtakes FIX camera | graphic impact

Picture: Torn asymmetric black and white shapes cross screen left to right with restrained cyan edges. No character or enemy appears.

### D06 | 13:16-15:00 | 32f | FIX transition plate

Picture: Residual horizontal speed lines clear into a controlled black field with one white torn edge exiting screen right.

Action: Hold enough clean negative space for editorial transition to a separately produced enemy reaction, block, survive, or fall scene.

Sound: High-frequency tail and three faint baby-dragon chirps from far behind.

## Layer Handoff

- CHAR-A, CHAR-B, CHAR-C: independent character layers with fixed trio spacing.
- NEST: woven prop plus one rebound deformation pass.
- BG-PREPARE: sparse loopable radial line field.
- BG-INHALE: loopable inward-converging line field.
- BG-RELEASE: short outward burst, not a loop.
- BG-TRAVEL: loopable horizontal line field.
- FX-SONIC-FRONT: broken planar refraction edge.
- FX-IMPACT: two to five frame black-white-cyan graphic drawings.
- SHADOW: restrained contact-shadow layer only.
- EDIT-HANDOFF: clean final transition plate for reusable enemy outcomes.
